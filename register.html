How to Apply

The Admission Process

Follow the registration stages carefully. Each stage helps ensure that student information is accurate and properly reviewed.

01

Start Registration

Open the student registration page and provide the requested personal information.

02

Academic Record

Provide the required previous academic information so the school can review your placement.

03

Fee Payment

Follow the official payment instructions and submit the required payment information for verification.

04

Review and Approval

The school reviews the registration and payment. Account access follows successful approval.

Student Information

Applicants should be prepared to provide accurate information, which may include:

  • Full legal name
  • Date of birth and contact information
  • Previous school information
  • Previous academic records when required

Academic Placement

Grade placement should be based on the student's verified academic history and the school's review process.

  • Previous grade and academic results
  • Promotion or detention status
  • Applicable school placement requirements
  • Stream placement for Grades 11 and 12

💳 Important: School Fee Payment

School-fee payment is a required part of registration. Payment information must be submitted through the official registration process and verified by the school's authorized payment-verification process.

Submitting payment details or a receipt does not automatically activate a student account. Registration remains pending until the required payment and school approval checks are completed.

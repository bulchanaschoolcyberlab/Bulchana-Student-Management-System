Our Academic Program

Education That Opens Doors

Our academic program focuses on helping students develop a strong foundation, understand the world around them, and prepare for opportunities in higher education and beyond.

Through classroom learning, practical activities, teacher guidance, and technology-supported education, we aim to make learning meaningful and connected to real life.

  • ✓ Structured learning for Grades 9–12
  • ✓ Guidance for academic development
  • ✓ Natural Science and Social Science pathways
  • ✓ Focus on knowledge, skills, and responsibility
Grade Levels

Our Academic Journey

Students progress through four high-school grade levels, building their knowledge and preparing for the next stage of education.

09

Grade 9 — Building Foundations

Students strengthen their academic foundations and develop essential learning habits as they begin high school.

  • Developing core subject knowledge
  • Building independent study habits
  • Improving communication and problem-solving
  • Exploring different areas of knowledge
10

Grade 10 — Strengthening Skills

Students deepen their understanding, improve their academic performance, and prepare for future academic pathways.

  • Strengthening subject understanding
  • Developing analytical thinking
  • Preparing for the next academic stage
  • Recognizing personal interests and strengths
11

Grade 11 — Choosing a Pathway

Students enter a more focused stage of learning through Natural Science or Social Science, according to the school's academic requirements and placement process.

  • Focused subject learning
  • Developing deeper academic understanding
  • Connecting learning with future goals
  • Preparing for advanced studies
12

Grade 12 — Preparing for the Future

Students strengthen their knowledge and prepare for important academic examinations and future educational opportunities.

  • Advanced learning in the selected pathway
  • Revision and examination preparation
  • Independent study and research skills
  • Planning for further education
Academic Pathways

Two Pathways. Different Opportunities.

In Grades 11 and 12, students follow a pathway that supports their academic interests and future goals.

🔬

Natural Science

A pathway for students interested in understanding nature, scientific principles, mathematics, technology, and the world through investigation and evidence.

SCIENCE · DISCOVERY · INNOVATION
🌍

Social Science

A pathway for students interested in people, societies, history, geography, human behavior, communication, and the forces that shape our world.

SOCIETY · CULTURE · UNDERSTANDING
